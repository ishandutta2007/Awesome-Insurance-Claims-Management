# Awesome-Insurance-Claims-Management

## Top Insurance Claims Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on FNOL, Claims Adjudication, Reserving, SIU, Payments & Claims Lifecycle Automation*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Insurance Claims Management**. These systems support the end-to-end claims lifecycle—from first notice of loss (FNOL) through investigation, adjudication, reserving, payment, and recovery—for P&C and other lines of business.



**Examples** include Guidewire ClaimCenter, Duck Creek Claims, Sapiens ClaimsPro, Majesco Claims, Snapsheet, Claimable, Insurity Claims, OneShield Claims, Origami Risk Claims, and CCS ClaimSuite (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full core claims systems are almost entirely commercial. Practical open options are limited to **self-insurance / mutual claims experiments**, **case-management frameworks**, and analytics/lakehouse projects for claims data. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Guidewire ClaimCenter](https://www.guidewire.com/)**  

  Industry-leading core claims platform for large P&C carriers, offering deep configurability, reserving, and integration within the Guidewire InsuranceSuite.



- **[Duck Creek Claims](https://www.duckcreek.com/)**  

  Modern SaaS-oriented claims platform for mid-market and larger carriers, emphasizing low-code configuration and cloud delivery.



- **[Sapiens ClaimsPro](https://www.sapiens.com/)**  

  Claims management solution from Sapiens supporting multi-line insurance claims processing and digital claims experiences.



- **[Majesco Claims](https://www.majesco.com/)**  

  Claims platform within Majesco’s insurance core suite for carriers seeking modern claims capabilities and digital engagement.



- **[Snapsheet](https://www.snapsheet.com/)**  

  Digital claims platform focused on virtual estimating, photo-based claims, and streamlined claims experiences for auto and related lines.



- **[Claimable](https://www.claimable.com/)**  

  Claims management software aimed at insurers, MGAs, and TPAs seeking modern, configurable claims workflows.



- **[Insurity Claims](https://www.insurity.com/)**  

  Claims capabilities within Insurity’s insurance technology portfolio for property & casualty carriers and programs.



- **[OneShield Claims](https://www.oneshield.com/)**  

  Configurable claims management solution as part of the OneShield insurance platform suite.



- **[Origami Risk Claims](https://www.origamirisk.com/)**  

  Risk and claims management platform used by insurers, captives, and self-insured organizations for claims and incident handling.



- **[CCS ClaimSuite / similar specialized claims suites](https://www.example.com/)**  

  Specialized claims management suites serving particular markets, TPAs, or lines of business.

  Open-source experiments in policy and claims management for group or mutual-style insurance, including claim evidence and settlement workflows.



- **[Open case management and workflow engines](https://github.com/)**  

  General open case-management and BPM tools adapted for claims intake, assignment, and investigation tracking.



- **[Insurance claims analytics / lakehouse open projects](https://github.com/)**  

  Open data pipelines and lakehouse patterns for claims analytics, reporting, and fraud/feature engineering research.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo case and project modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Open ERP modules that can be configured for claim-like cases, tasks, documents, and basic financials.



- **[Document intake and IDP open pipelines](https://github.com/)**  

  Open OCR and document-processing stacks used to support FNOL document capture and classification.



- **[Rules engines for adjudication open libraries](https://github.com/)**  

  Open business-rules engines that teams experiment with for simple coverage and payment decision logic.



- **[Payment and ledger open components](https://github.com/)**  

  Open accounting and payment-tracking tools sometimes paired with claims prototypes for indemnity flows.



- **[SIU / investigation case open trackers](https://github.com/)**

- **[Customer notification and portal open kits](https://github.com/)**

- **[Synthetic claims data generators](https://github.com/)**

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **open case/workflow tools** for intake and task tracking in small or captive environments.

- Building **claims analytics** on open lakehouse stacks while keeping core adjudication in a commercial system.

- Experimenting with **mutual/self-insurance open platforms** for niche community insurance models.

- Accepting that production core claims (FNOL, coverage verification, reserving, regulatory reporting, carrier-scale workflows) still require commercial platforms (Guidewire ClaimCenter, Duck Creek Claims, Sapiens, Majesco, Snapsheet, Insurity, OneShield, Origami Risk, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on analytics transparency, document processing, and claimant experience layers.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Capture FNOL via forms or portals → manage cases in an open workflow tool → store documents under controlled repositories → run analytics on an open lakehouse → escalate complex adjudication to a commercial claims core. Suitable mainly for captives, TPAs with limited scope, or research. Most carriers standardize on commercial claims management platforms for regulatory and operational scale.
